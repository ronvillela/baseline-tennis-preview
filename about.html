Hi, I’m Vittorio.

My Coaching Philosophy

Understand It. Practice It. Use It.

I believe coaching should give you something you can understand and put into practice. That means clear feedback, purposeful repetition and chances to use what you learn in real points.

Some days we focus on a technical adjustment. Other days we work on movement, shot selection or confidence during match play. The session follows what your game needs.

How We Train

My Coaching Approach

Technique

Build efficient, repeatable strokes.

Movement

Better positioning, balance and court coverage.

Strategy

Understand patterns, point construction and decision-making.

Match Play

Learn how to actually use your game under pressure.

Who I Work With

From Your First Rally to Your Next Challenge

I coach adults and juniors, from beginner through advanced. Whether you’re learning the basics, returning to tennis or preparing for competitive points, we start with your goals.

  • Private lessons — focused one-on-one attention.
  • Semi-private lessons — shared training for two players.
  • Clinics — maximum four players, with individual feedback.
  • Hitting / sparring — live-ball practice and match preparation.

Why Train With Me?

You’ll work directly with me on the details that matter to your game. We connect technique and movement with strategy and match play, so practice has a purpose beyond the drill.

Choose the format that suits you, with coaching across Miami and clear feedback throughout your session.
