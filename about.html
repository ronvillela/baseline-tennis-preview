About Vittorio Zecca

Passionate about the game and dedicated to helping every player reach their potential — on court and beyond.

Coach Photo

Personal Instruction Built Around the Player

Vittorio Zecca provides personalized tennis instruction for players who want a clear, encouraging path to improvement.

Every session is shaped around the player’s current level, goals, and learning style, with attention to fundamentals, movement, consistency, and practical match application.

Whether you are picking up a racquet for the first time or working to sharpen an established game, the objective is the same: build confidence, strengthen repeatable skills, and make progress that carries onto the court.

Coaching Focus

Strong Foundations

Build dependable technique, movement patterns, and habits that support long-term improvement.

Practical Development

Translate instruction into drills, point play, and situations that reflect how tennis is actually played.

Individual Progress

Lessons are adjusted to the player’s level, goals, pace, and preferred learning style.

Coaching Philosophy

The Baseline Tennis approach is centered on steady progress: clear instruction, purposeful repetition, and practical goals that keep each lesson focused and encouraging.

Baseline Tennis was founded on the belief that premium coaching should be accessible, personal, and rooted in a genuine respect for the sport. That's the standard you'll experience from your very first lesson.
