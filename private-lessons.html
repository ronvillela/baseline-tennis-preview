Private Tennis Lessons

A focused 60-minute session built around your technique, movement, consistency, tactics, and personal goals.

Focused Instruction, Personalized to You

Private lessons give each player the time and attention to understand what is happening in their game and make practical corrections. Sessions may include technical drills, movement patterns, live-ball work, point construction, serving, returning, and match strategy.

What May Be Included

  • Stroke evaluation and technical corrections
  • Footwork and court movement
  • Serve and return development
  • Consistency, placement, and point construction
  • Slow-motion video analysis
  • Progress tracking and personalized development goals
