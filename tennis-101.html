A welcoming, structured introduction for players with little or no tennis experience.

Learn the Game from the Ground Up

Tennis 101 gives beginners the essential skills and understanding needed to enjoy the court with confidence.

  • Proper grips
  • Forehand fundamentals
  • Backhand fundamentals
  • Volleys
  • Serve fundamentals
  • Footwork & movement
  • Tennis scoring and rules
  • Singles & doubles basics
  • Rally consistency
  • Court positioning
Program Goal

By the end of Tennis 101, students should feel confident rallying, serving, keeping score, and playing recreational matches.
