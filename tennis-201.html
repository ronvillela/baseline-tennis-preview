Intermediate Program

Tennis 201

You've learned the basics — now it's time to compete with confidence. Tennis 201 sharpens your strokes, deepens your tactics, and prepares you for club play and local tournaments.

Program Overview

Tennis 201 is designed for players who can rally consistently and understand basic scoring but want to take their game to the next level. The curriculum focuses on stroke refinement, tactical patterns, and competitive match play.

  • Topspin and slice variations on groundstrokes
  • Serve placement, kick serve introduction, and return patterns
  • Approach shots, volleys, and transition game
  • Singles and doubles strategy and court positioning
  • Mental game — handling pressure and constructing points
  • Match preparation and tournament readiness

Prerequisites

Players should be able to rally from the baseline, serve with reasonable consistency, and keep score independently. If you're not there yet, start with Tennis 101 or a few private lessons first.

Format Options

Tennis 201 runs as a six-week group series and is also available as a private lesson track for players who prefer individualized pacing. Many participants combine weekly group sessions with an occasional private lesson for targeted work.
