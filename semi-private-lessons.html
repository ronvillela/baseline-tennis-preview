Semi-Private Tennis Lessons

Share the court, split the cost, and still get the focused coaching you need. Semi-private lessons are perfect for pairs who want to improve together.

The Best of Both Worlds

Semi-private lessons bring together the personalization of private coaching with the energy and accountability of learning alongside someone else. Each player receives individual feedback while benefiting from live-ball drills and cooperative exercises.

  • Designed for two players of similar or complementary skill levels
  • Live-ball rallying and point-play scenarios
  • Doubles-specific strategy when both players compete together
  • More affordable per person than a full private lesson
  • Fun, motivating environment that keeps you coming back

Popular Pairings

  • Spouses or partners learning together
  • Parent and teen building a shared activity
  • Doubles partners sharpening communication and positioning
  • Friends at a similar level looking to improve

Session Details

Sessions run 60 or 90 minutes and can be booked as a single lesson or a recurring weekly slot. Both players should register together when booking online.
