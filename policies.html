Cancellation, Rain & Refund Policy

Please review these policies before booking lessons, packages, or clinics.

01

Cancellation Policy

Lessons canceled at least 24 hours in advance may be rescheduled at no additional charge.

Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled lesson may be charged the full lesson fee.

02

Rain Policy

Outdoor lessons affected by rain or unsafe weather will be rescheduled at the next available opportunity.

03

Refund Policy

Completed lessons are non-refundable.

Unused lesson packages may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis under exceptional circumstances.
