Policies — Draft for Coach Review

Scheduling & Payment

Online scheduling and payment are not connected yet. For now, lesson and clinic requests can be made by phone, text, email, or through the contact form.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

The final cancellation and rescheduling policy will be added after Vittorio confirms the required notice period and any applicable fees.

Weather

Outdoor sessions may need to be adjusted because of rain, lightning, or unsafe court conditions. The final weather and makeup-session process will be confirmed before launch.

What to Bring

Players should plan to bring appropriate athletic clothing, court shoes, water, sun protection, and a racquet. Contact Vittorio before the first lesson with any equipment questions.

Junior Players

A parent or guardian should contact Vittorio before a junior player’s first session so supervision, waiver, and court arrangements can be confirmed.
