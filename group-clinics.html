Group Tennis Clinics

Learn alongside other motivated players in small-group clinics that combine instruction, fitness, and the camaraderie of shared improvement.

Clinic Format

Group clinics keep player counts intentionally small — typically four to six participants — so everyone gets meaningful reps and individual feedback. Each session follows a structured warm-up, technical focus, and live-ball segment.

  • Weekly themed clinics (serve, volley, groundstrokes, match play)
  • Skill-based grouping so you're matched with similar players
  • Cardio tennis elements for fitness and endurance
  • Point-play and competitive drills to apply what you learn
  • Affordable per-session pricing with multi-week packages

Current Clinic Themes

  • Serve & Return Clinic — Build a reliable first serve and aggressive return game
  • Net Game Intensive — Volleys, overheads, and transition footwork
  • Baseline Rally Clinic — Consistency, spin, and directional control
  • Match Play Friday — Structured singles and doubles point play
